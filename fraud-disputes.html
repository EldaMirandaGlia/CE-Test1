Fraud prevention / Learn

A few habits worth keeping.

Preventive education complements the report, dispute, and case-management workflows above.

✉

Recognize phishing

Question urgent requests sent by text, email, or social media. Navigate to a bank through a verified channel you already trust.

◇

Guard your identity

Limit the sharing of sensitive identifiers. Review reports and follow established procedures if you suspect identity theft.

✓

Use stronger access

Use unique passwords, multifactor authentication, alerts, and prompt reviews of genuine account activity.

For authentic US identity-theft recovery information, consult IdentityTheft.gov. NexusBank cannot receive reports or provide financial support.
