How to think about it

Beyond the interest rate.

A comparison should consider more than a single monthly payment.

01

Time horizon

A 15-year term may cost more each month but less interest across the scheduled loan lifetime.

02

Cash at closing

A larger down payment changes your principal; real purchases can involve extra cash for closing costs.

03

Whole-home budget

Taxes, insurance, upkeep, and any HOA dues also matter when evaluating affordability.

Frequently considered

Questions worth asking.

This quick primer explains how our simulator frames common mortgage topics.

What is the difference between interest rate and APR?

The interest rate describes borrowing cost on principal. APR can incorporate certain fees and points, making it useful for comparisons. This simulator models an example interest rate only.

Why does a smaller down payment change the estimate?

It increases the amount financed, and this demo adds a simple PMI assumption below 20% down. Actual insurance requirements and pricing vary.

Can I apply for a NexusBank mortgage?

No. NexusBank is fictional. No application, preapproval, underwriting, or funds are offered through this site.
